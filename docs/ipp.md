# Industrial Progress Phase (IPP)

Every machine and technology in **DegeneracyCraft** belongs to one of **eleven Industrial Progress Phases (IPP)**.

Unlike conventional technology mods where progression is measured only by stronger machines, IPP represents the **qualitative evolution of technology** and the scale at which it can influence reality itself.

Advancement through IPP is not a matter of quantitative improvement, but a qualitative expansion of the very layers of reality upon which technology can act.

## Progression

Industrial Progress Phase represents **how far technology has evolved** rather than **how powerful a machine has become**.

Its progression can be viewed as an ever-expanding sphere of technological influence.

```text
Machine
        ↓
Factory
        ↓
Civilization
        ↓
Planet
        ↓
Star System
        ↓
Galaxy
        ↓
Universe
        ↓
Parallel Universes
        ↓
Infinity
```

Every machine in DegeneracyCraft belongs to **both** an **Academic Field** and an **Industrial Progress Phase**.

The Academic Field defines **what** the technology studies.

The Industrial Progress Phase defines **how far** that technology has evolved.

Together, these two systems form the foundation of DegeneracyCraft's technological progression.
